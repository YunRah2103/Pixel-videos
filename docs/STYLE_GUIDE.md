# Visual direction: illustrated cinematic pixel art

Reference feeling: quiet, magical, emotionally resonant environments (pastel sunset coastlines, teal-blue rainy streets, illuminated bedrooms, trains by rivers, lush night forests). A restrained colour palette and considered framing matter more than raw polygon count.

**Look:** crisp deliberate pixels, varied silhouette scale, rich foreground details, layered atmospheric depth, lively yet subtle motion, tasteful warm light against cool shadows, textured reflections, detailed architecture and vegetation. Small characters and passing vehicles make the world feel inhabited.

**Movement:** 2–5 independent environmental loops per shot, ideally clouds, rain, water, foliage, flickering lights, drifting mist, sprite animation. Prefer slow physical camera motion over excessive transitions.

**Avoid:** simply applying a pixelate filter to an ugly model; sterile empty landscapes; jagged inconsistent pixel scales; type-heavy overlays; random scene changes; unlicensed sprites; GIF-looking jittery animation; hard-coded real time / non-determinism.

**Quality bar:** a screenshot of almost any frame should be attractive enough to serve as a standalone wallpaper; motion should elevate the image rather than hide weak artwork.
