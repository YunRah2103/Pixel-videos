# Pixel Studio V5 — No Extra AI Payments

All new tools are free or open source. No AI generation credits, subscription or paid export.

Existing verified V4: Pixelorama, Krita, LDtk, Godot, Pixel Snapper, Sharp, PixiJS, Remotion and FFmpeg.

New: SpookyGhost official MIT Linux binary from its GitHub artifact repository; Tiled free AppImage; LibreSprite open-source AppImage; Laigter Linux AppImage v1.13.1 (current 1.14 has no Linux binary).

Artist-made artwork: Ansimuz Cyberpunk Street from OpenGameArt is CC-BY-3.0 **requiring attribution**. Kenney Tiny City is CC0. Lospec Apollo palette is free to retrieve from its API. The different itch.io Synth Cities release is listed CC0 but is only available through its download flow; do not claim that one was imported.

## Usage on Linux x86_64

    python3 scripts/setup_pixel_art_v5.py --all
    python3 scripts/setup_pixel_art_v5.py --check

Binaries and artist assets live in ignored .tools/pixel-v5. Actions runners download them fresh; none are committed to GitHub.

## Five-second test

After downloading artwork, use python3 scripts/render_v5_art_test.py. Publish credit for Ansimuz, linking https://opengameart.org/content/cyberpunk-street-environment and CC BY 3.0.

CRITICAL: Download does not prove use. Do not claim SpookyGhost, Laigter, Pixelorama, Tiled or LibreSprite were used to create frames unless you provide actual edit/export evidence. A GIF or still panning sideways is not an impressive animation.
